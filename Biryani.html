How dum biryani is made

Dum means "to breathe in" and "to steam". The pot is sealed so every grain cooks in the flavour of the meat below it.

Marinate overnight

Meat rests for 12 hours in hung curd, raw papaya, ginger-garlic and our house garam masala.

Par-boil the rice

Aged basmati goes into whole spices and salted water until it's 70 percent cooked, no further.

Layer the handi

Meat, rice, fried onions, mint, saffron milk and ghee go in layers. Nothing is stirred.

Seal and cook slow

A strip of dough locks the lid. The handi sits on low heat for 3 hours, then comes to your table sealed.
